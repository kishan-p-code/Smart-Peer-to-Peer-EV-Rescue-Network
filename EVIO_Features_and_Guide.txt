1. WHAT IS EVIO? (SIMPLE SUMMARY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EVIO is a complete smart platform for Electric Vehicles (EVs). It connects:
1. EV Drivers looking for nearby, fast, and available charging spots.
2. Station Hosts (homeowners, business owners, hotel managers) who want to share
   or rent out their EV chargers and earn money.
3. Grid Supervisors (electrical safety engineers and grid operators) who inspect
   chargers, balance power load on the electrical grid, and keep the system safe.
4. Commercial Franchise Partners who want to launch turnkey public charging hubs.

Unlike basic map apps that only show static pins, EVIO offers real-time slot
booking, automated energy telemetry, emergency battery delivery, peer-to-peer EV
charging, AI-driven trip simulation, and live electrical safety verification.

B. EMERGENCY & HIGHWAY SOS SERVICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Doorstep Swappable Battery Delivery:
   - For drivers stranded with 0% battery or low range who cannot reach a charger.
   - Pinpoints your breakdown coordinates via GPS.
   - Dispatches a mobile delivery van carrying a fully charged 100% battery pack.
   - Live status pipeline: Requested -> Dispatched -> Partner En Route -> Swapped.
   - Displays partner name, vehicle plate, and estimated time of arrival (~18 mins).

2. Highway Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) Reverse Charge SOS:
   - For highway emergencies where an EV is stranded between distant cities.
   - Broadcasts an emergency power request to nearby EV drivers with bidirectional
     V2L/V2V capabilities.
   - Passing EV donors can accept the request, hook up a Type 2 / CCS transfer cable,
     and transfer 5 to 15 kWh of energy directly from their car to yours.
   - Donor earns a compensation payout for the energy shared.
   - Real-time online donor driver radar map showing active EVs on the corridor.

3. NORMAL PLAIN-ENGLISH EXPLANATION OF HOW IT WORKS
================================================================================

SCENARIO 1: YOU ARE AN EV DRIVER NEEDING A CHARGE
-------------------------------------------------
1. Open the App: The map automatically pinpoints your current location with a blue
   pulsing dot.
2. Find a Charger: Look around your pin on the map. You will see several charging
   stations (green for available hubs, purple for fast public stations).
3. Check Distance and Specs: Each station card tells you exactly how many kilometers
   away it is, what speed it charges at (e.g., 22kW or 60kW), and the price per unit.
4. Book a Slot: Tap on a station you like, choose what time you want to arrive and
   how many hours you want to charge. Pay using your in-app wallet or card.
5. Plug In & Track: When you arrive, plug your car in and start the session. You can
   watch your car's battery percentage climb on your phone screen in real-time,
   along with the exact electricity cost.
6. Stop and Go: When your car is full, tap "Stop Session", unplug, and receive your
   digital invoice right in the app.


SCENARIO 2: YOU ARE PLANNING A LONG HIGHWAY TRIP
-------------------------------------------------
1. Open "AI Journey Prediction" from the top menu.
2. Enter your starting point (or click "Use My GPS") and enter your destination.
3. Select your exact electric car model and your current battery percentage (e.g., 40%).
4. The system calculates the road distance and checks if your battery will make it.
5. If your car will run out of power along the highway, the app marks the danger point
   on the map and automatically suggests the best highway charging stop midway.
6. It tells you exactly how many minutes you need to stop for (e.g., 28 mins to reach 80%)
   and what the recharge will cost, so you never get stranded.


SCENARIO 3: YOU RUN OUT OF BATTERY ON THE ROAD (EMERGENCY)
----------------------------------------------------------
If you ever run out of charge away from a station, you have two safety nets:
1. Battery Delivery: Tap "Doorstep Swappable Battery Delivery". A mobile support van
   comes to your car's exact GPS location with a fresh battery pack to get you moving.
2. V2V Highway SOS: Tap "Highway V2V Reverse Charge SOS". The app alerts other EV
   drivers on the same highway. A nearby driver can pull over, connect a transfer
   cable between cars, and share some battery power with you.


SCENARIO 4: YOU HAVE AN EV CHARGER AT HOME OR BUSINESS AND WANT TO EARN MONEY
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Switch your role to "Host" in the top bar.
2. Click "List a Charger". Enter your address, charger speed (e.g., 7.4kW or 22kW),
   and the price you want to charge per kWh.
3. Upload a photo and your electrician's safety inspection certificate.
4. Once our grid supervisor verifies the safety document, your charger goes live
   on the public map.
5. When local drivers charge at your bay, payments are transferred straight into
   your wallet. You can set it to auto-accept bookings or approve them manually.


SCENARIO 5: KEEPING THE ELECTRICAL GRID SAFE (SUPERVISOR ROLE)
-------------------------------------------------------------
Electric vehicle charging takes a massive amount of electrical power. If too many
cars charge on one neighborhood transformer at once, fuses blow and power goes out.
1. Grid Supervisors use the "Supervisor Console" to monitor live voltage, frequency,
   and electrical load.
2. When high peak hours happen, EVIO's load balancer automatically slows down non-urgent
   charging to protect the neighborhood grid.
3. Supervisors also inspect every single charger before it goes live to make sure
   the electrical earthing is safe and won't cause shocks or fires.

4. KEY TECHNICAL STANDARDS FOLLOWED BY EVIO
================================================================================
- IEC 61851-1: International standard for EV conductive charging systems.
- NB 777 / National Discom Code: Earthing resistance compliance (< 5 Ohms).
- RCD Type B: Residual Current Devices providing AC and DC leakage protection.
- Open Charge Map (OCM) API: Global registry integration for public charging points.
- Leaflet & OpenStreetMap: High-performance, open-standard interactive geospatial mapping.
- Nominatim Geocoding: Automated geographic coordinate resolution for trip planning.
